Why it works

The mechanics that decide this build

SLOTS
Max sockets a Witch can open: two-hander 5, bow 5, one-hander 3, shield 2, chest 3, gloves 2, boots 2, helm 1. Cloaks and accessories take none. The Kuku resistance chests are fixed at 0. Embedding and extracting are free; only opening sockets costs silver (about 105 for all five on one item). tested
LOCAL
Attack and Crit on a weapon count only for that weapon. On gloves and boots they count only for punches and kicks, so Insight or Destruction in gloves is wasted for a sword build. tested
GLOBAL
Necklace, rings and the Kuku Flame-Resistant chest give Crit and Attack to every weapon. Attack Speed from any armor piece or ring is global; on a weapon or shield it works only while that item is in hand. Defense and Movement Speed are global from everything, including a shield stowed on your back. tested
CAPS
Crit, Attack Speed and Movement Speed hard-cap at Lv15. The game's own status table holds a 16-step curve per stat; Crit and Attack Speed share the same diminishing curve, Movement Speed is linear to Lv6 then bends. Levels 1 to 5 deliver 47% of the cap's value, levels 11 to 15 only 22%. data
TRIGGERS
Every gear bound to the same input fires together: Groundsurge and Greysoul Howling both fire on Turning Slash (R1+R2), Shadow Claw and Crow's Pursuit on Heavy Attack / Forward Slash (R2). Volcanic Eruption and Flames of Judgment fire on any flame-imbued attack, which includes an imbued Turning Slash and an imbued Charged Shot. data
SPIRIT
Turning Slash costs 10 Spirit, 20 when imbued; each skill gear on the input adds its own cost. Shadow Claw and Crow's Pursuit draw Stamina instead, so the two-hander splits its burst across both pools. tested

Attack vs crit

Where the "53 attack" rule comes from, and where it stops

A crit is a flat 2× hit, so expected damage is Attack × (1 + crit chance). One more Attack point is worth 1/Attack. One more crit level is worth its slice of the curve divided by (1 + crit chance). At base crit the curve's first steps are big, so a crit level beats an attack point once total attack passes roughly 50. Past Lv10 the curve flattens and the attack point wins again. Move the sliders.

What this means for socket choices

Get your crit from sources that cost no weapon socket: the weapon's own crit line, the Kuku Flame-Resistant chest (Lv3) and a Lv4 necklace. That is Lv7 to Lv11 for free. From there, every spare weapon socket is Destruction III (+3 Attack), not Insight III, because +3 Attack on a 50-plus total is a 5 to 6% gain and three more crit levels at Lv11 add about 4%.

The same curve applies to Attack Speed. Lv10 captures about 78% of the cap's benefit, which is why the loadout spends only one socket on Swift and takes the rest from innate gloves and a ring.

Caveat: if enemy defense is a flat subtraction rather than a percentage, each attack point is worth even more than this model says. Either way the conclusion (crit from free sources, attack in sockets) holds.

Abyss Tree

Skills the loadouts depend on

Melee (Stamina tree)

  1. Turning Slash Lv3 with Proficiency, Expertise and Rend Armor. Proficiency lets other skills chain into it, so a Shadow Claw Forward Slash flows straight into a Groundsurge + Greysoul Turning Slash.
  2. Forward Slash Lv3 with Proficiency and Sure Hit. This is the Shadow Claw button.
  3. Armed Combat Lv5 for Quick Swap (L1+L2): switch between the two-hander and the sword pair with an attack during the swap. Lv6 adds Finishing Rush (2.0).
  4. Spinning Slash Lv2 + Proficiency only if you run Hound's Claws on a sword.

Spirit and Health trees

  1. Nature's Echo (Keen Senses Lv3 + Forward Slash Lv3) then Echoing Forward Slash: a free illusion copies every Forward Slash.
  2. Imbue Element: unlocked by clearing the Spire of Ringing Truth chain (Frost) and continuing through Sanctorum of Darkness to the Tree of Slumber for Flame Strike. Raise Imbue Element to Lv4 for Elemental Turning Slash (Lv2 gives Elemental Charged Shot for the bow). This is the switch that turns on Volcanic Eruption and Flames of Judgment.
  3. Focus Lv4 (Serenity, 2.0) for Spirit regen during Focus; Keen Senses Lv4 (Enraged Parry, 2.0) to parry mid-combo.
  4. Bow: Charged Shot, Evasive Shot Lv3 + Expertise (fires two arrows), Focused Shot, Marksmanship maxed.
  5. Core nodes: Stamina first, then Health, then Spirit.
Acquisition

Where everything comes from, with story gates

ItemSourceGate

Every piece reaches +10 with 1 Aeserion's Scale + 1 Abyss Artifact at the last step, and 1 Abyss Artifact per step from +5. Budget roughly 16 pieces × 6 Artifacts and 16 Scales for the full set; the +10 step also raises innate Crit or Attack Speed by one level on weapons that have it.
